Astronaut Health Monitor in 30 seconds

Team Spades · 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge · Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions

About this video

A one-minute daily health check for astronauts far from Earth that decides whether a problem can wait for Earth's reply. Captions are burned in (English). Length: 29 seconds. Pictures are real screenshots of the app.

AI disclosure: the narration voice is AI-generated with Kokoro-82M text-to-speech (voice am_michael), run locally. It is labelled on screen throughout and recorded in the file's metadata. The app code was written with Claude (Anthropic), directed by the team. All crew and readings are simulated; the app does not diagnose. No NASA logos, flags or mission identifiers are used.
