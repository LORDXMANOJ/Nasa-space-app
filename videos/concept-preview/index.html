Day one-twenty of a Mars transit.
The nearest doctor is twelve light-minutes away.
Astronaut Health Monitor lets the crew check, understand,
and act on their own health. Completely offline.
Every check is guided: why it matters in space,
a short how-to, then a reading explained in plain English.
The body report shows where.
After five nights of poor sleep, the brain flags Act,
and the heart turns to Watch.
A recovery plan follows. Sleep, exercise, a crew check-in,
and a clear rule for when to call the flight surgeon.
One click sends a report to Earth, light-delay and all.
Days later, everything is back to Nominal.
Your health, readable, even millions of kilometres from home.
